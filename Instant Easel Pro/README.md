# Artwork gallery

Every illustration and character page for the trilogy. Click any image to open the full-size PNG (1200×1800) and download it.

**Final artwork so far (supplied by the author; originals in `source/`):** `b1-00-1` (Book One, Prologue: the First Ones' city at night) and `b1-01-1` (Book One, Chapter 1: the summit). All other images are **painterly placeholders**: code-drawn scenes with rounded, organic shapes, repainted by `paint.py` in brushstrokes, with skies, ground and rock borrowed from figure-free parts of the author's paintings, and color-matched to them. The plain originals are in `out_placeholders/`.

High-resolution print versions (1800×2700) are in `out/` and inside the PDFs in `Publication Edition/`.
